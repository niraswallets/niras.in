Privacy Policy

Welcome to Nira's Wallets. Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to protecting the personal information you share while using our website, www.niras.in. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information whenever you visit our website, browse our products, create an account, subscribe to our newsletter, or place an order. By accessing or using our website, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.


Information We Collect

When you interact with Nira's Wallets, we may collect personal information including your name, email address, phone number, shipping and billing address, payment-related details, and any other information you voluntarily provide during checkout, customer support inquiries, newsletter subscriptions, or promotional activities. We may also collect non-personal information such as your browser type, device information, operating system, IP address, pages visited, time spent on our website, and other technical data to improve your browsing experience.


How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to process and deliver your orders, verify transactions, provide customer support, send order confirmations and shipping updates, respond to your inquiries, improve our website, personalize your shopping experience, prevent fraudulent activities, and comply with applicable legal obligations. If you subscribe to our newsletter, we may send updates about new product launches, exclusive offers, promotions, and important announcements. You may unsubscribe from these communications at any time.


Order Processing

To fulfill your orders efficiently, we may use your personal information for payment verification, inventory management, shipping coordination, and communication regarding your purchase. Accurate information helps us ensure timely delivery and provide a seamless shopping experience.


Payment Security

Nira's Wallets values the security of your financial information. Payments made through our website are processed using trusted and secure payment service providers. We do not store your complete debit card, credit card, UPI PIN, CVV, or online banking credentials on our servers. All payment transactions are encrypted using industry-standard security technologies.


Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance website performance, remember your preferences, improve functionality, analyze website traffic, and provide a personalized shopping experience. Cookies help us understand customer behavior and continuously improve our services. You may choose to disable cookies through your browser settings; however, some website features may not function properly.


Information Sharing

Nira's Wallets does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. We may share limited information only with trusted service providers involved in payment processing, order fulfillment, logistics, website maintenance, analytics, fraud prevention, and legal compliance. These partners are obligated to protect your information and use it solely for the services they provide on our behalf.


Data Protection

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, misuse, alteration, or destruction. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can guarantee absolute security.


Marketing Communications

If you choose to receive promotional communications from Nira's Wallets, we may occasionally send emails regarding exclusive offers, seasonal collections, discounts, product launches, and lifestyle updates. You may opt out of these communications at any time by following the unsubscribe instructions included in our emails.


Third-Party Services

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, payment gateways, or external services. These websites operate independently and have their own privacy practices. Nira's Wallets is not responsible for the privacy policies, content, or security of third-party websites, and we encourage users to review their respective privacy policies before sharing any personal information.


Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for individuals who are legally capable of entering into binding agreements. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the applicable legal age. If we become aware that such information has been collected inadvertently, we will take appropriate steps to remove it promptly.


Your Rights

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you, request corrections to inaccurate information, or ask us to delete your personal information where permitted by applicable law. You may also contact us if you have questions regarding the handling of your personal data.


Changes to This Privacy Policy

Nira's Wallets reserves the right to modify or update this Privacy Policy at any time without prior notice. Any changes will become effective immediately upon being published on this page. We encourage customers to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect their information.
